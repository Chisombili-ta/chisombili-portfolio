Where the numbers meet the decision

Accounting discipline, applied to business intelligence.

Chisombili Aniebue is a Business Intelligence & Data Analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria. She trained as an accountant — B.Sc in Accounting from the University of Abuja, 3.93 GPA — before moving into analytics, and that background shows up in how she treats data: reconciled, sourced, and traceable before it ever reaches a dashboard.

At FiberOne Broadband, she started as a data analyst intern and was converted to full-time within four months. Today she builds and maintains the Power BI reporting that procurement, finance, and customer operations rely on day to day — from raw extraction through to the KPI scorecards leadership reads.

She works across SQL, Power BI, DAX, Power Query, R, and Power Automate, and is most interested in the handoff between clean data and a decision someone can actually act on.
