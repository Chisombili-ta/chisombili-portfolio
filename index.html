Accounting discipline, applied to business intelligence.

Chisombili Aniebue is a Business Intelligence & Data Analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria. She trained as an accountant holding a B.Sc in Accounting from the University of Abuja, before moving into analytics, and that background shows up in how she treats data: reconciled, sourced, and traceable before it ever reaches a dashboard.

At FiberOne Broadband, she started as a data analyst intern and was converted to full-time within four months. Today she builds and maintains the Power BI reporting that procurement, finance, and customer operations rely on day to day.
